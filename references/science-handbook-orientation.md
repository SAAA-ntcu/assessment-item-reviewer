# Science curriculum handbook orientation

Secondary source basis: `自然科學領域課程手冊(定稿版).pdf` supplied by the user. Treat the handbook as an interpretive/reference source and the formal curriculum as authoritative.

Use these notes only when they materially help a general review or a targeted curriculum claim.

## Interpretation principles relevant to assessment review

- Natural-science learning is not only isolated factual recall. The handbook emphasizes the relationship among learning performance, learning content, inquiry, and scientific attitudes/nature of science.
- Elementary science emphasizes exploration, observation, firsthand experience, and concrete operation; junior-high learning increasingly connects systematic scientific concepts, abstract thinking, experimentation, and inquiry.
- Learning performance and learning content should be read together when the report makes an explicit curriculum claim.
- The handbook emphasizes coherent concept development across stages rather than treating every content fragment as an isolated checklist item.

## Assessment orientation

The handbook presents multiple assessment approaches for inquiry-related learning, including text products, operation/performance, oral/product presentation, experiment planning/reporting, and portfolio-style evidence.

For this Skill:

- do not require every standardized item to assess full inquiry performance;
- do check whether a report overstates what a selected-response item actually demonstrates about experiment design, inquiry, evidence, or scientific reasoning;
- when the item itself contains data, an experiment, a figure, variables, or evidence, check whether the report's explanation preserves the actual causal/measurement structure;
- when a concrete learning-performance/content code or stage claim is cited, verify it against `curriculum-science-2018.pdf` and use this file as secondary interpretation.

## Important caution on achievement labels

The handbook states that the natural-science curriculum itself does not divide learning performance into labels such as「基礎」「待加強」「精熟」. If a report uses such labels, treat them as assessment-program/report categories unless another source defines them; do not attribute those categories to the curriculum without evidence.

## Review cautions

- Do not automatically convert a general report review into a specialist audit of physics, chemistry, biology, or earth science.
- Do flag obvious item/report contradictions, incorrect variable roles, unsupported cause-effect statements, mismatched evidence and conclusion, or terminology errors that can be established from the item and supplied sources.
- If resolution requires specialist scientific content beyond the supplied sources, mark it for subject-expert verification.
