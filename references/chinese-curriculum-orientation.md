# Chinese curriculum orientation

Primary source bundled in this Skill: `curriculum-chinese-2018.pdf` (十二年國民基本教育課程綱要－語文領域－國語文，民國107年1月).

Use this orientation to recognize the structure of the official curriculum during general review. For an exact code, stage, or wording claim, inspect the bundled official PDF rather than relying only on this summary.

## Broad structure

The official curriculum organizes learning focus through both learning performance and learning content.

Learning-performance categories are:

- listening;
- oral expression;
- phonetic symbols and use;
- character recognition and writing;
- reading;
- writing.

Stage coding is:

- I: elementary Grades 1-2;
- II: elementary Grades 3-4;
- III: elementary Grades 5-6;
- IV: junior-high Grades 7-9;
- V: senior-high Grades 10-12.

Learning content includes three broad themes: text/language units, modes of textual expression, and cultural content. The curriculum is designed so learning performance and learning content can be combined rather than assumed to form a rigid one-to-one mapping.

## General-review use

During default review:

- prioritize whether the item, objective, report analysis, distractor explanation, statistics, and teaching recommendation form a coherent chain;
- recognize Chinese learning-performance codes and stage labels when they appear;
- do not force a mathematics-style one-code/one-grade interpretation;
- if the manuscript makes a concrete code, stage, or learning-content claim, verify it against `curriculum-chinese-2018.pdf`;
- use `chinese-handbook-orientation.md` only as secondary interpretation;
- if a claim requires literary, linguistic, or curriculum-specialist adjudication beyond the supplied sources, mark it for subject-expert verification.
