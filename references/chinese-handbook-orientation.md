# Chinese curriculum handbook orientation

Secondary source basis: `語文領域-國語文課程手冊(定稿版).pdf` supplied by the user. The official handbook is interpretive and reference-oriented; it does not override the formal curriculum.

Use these notes only when they materially help a general review or a targeted curriculum claim.

## Interpretation principles relevant to assessment review

- The handbook explains learning-performance meaning by educational stage so teachers and editors can grasp the intended scope more concretely.
- It intentionally preserves flexibility in curriculum/material interpretation; in its learning-focus analysis it does not provide a complete analysis of every learning-content item.
- Learning performance and learning content may have one-to-one, one-to-many, or many-to-many relationships. Do not assume a single fixed pairing.
- In teacher-facing review, check whether the manuscript's claimed assessment target is reasonably connected to the cited learning performance/content, not whether it reproduces one handbook example.

## Assessment orientation

The handbook emphasizes that assessment should:

- address knowledge/skills and also relevant attitudes when appropriate;
- consider both learning results and learning processes;
- use formative and summative information rather than treating one format as universally sufficient;
- examine whether students can integrate and apply learning in context;
- use formative and diagnostic assessment to inform teaching adjustment.

For this Skill, these ideas are guidance for judging whether a report's teaching recommendation and assessment interpretation are coherent. Do not use them to demand that every standardized item itself become a performance task.

## Review cautions

- Do not require every item to cover all six learning-performance categories.
- Do not infer that a handbook teaching example is the only valid teaching or assessment method.
- Do not force one learning-performance code to map to exactly one learning-content code.
- If the manuscript cites an exact official code or stage, verify against `curriculum-chinese-2018.pdf` first; use this handbook orientation second.
