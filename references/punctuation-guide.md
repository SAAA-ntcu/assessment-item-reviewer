# Punctuation Guide

## Use

Apply this guide to formal Chinese assessment-report prose. The bundled revised punctuation handbook is the source baseline. Review punctuation for function, placement, and document-level consistency without becoming a punctuation-only reviewer.

## Official baseline

The handbook covers fifteen punctuation types and explicitly states that where type A/type B alternatives are provided, either may be used. Therefore, an allowed alternative is not an error by itself.

Common forms relevant to reports include:

- sentence stop: `。`
- comma: `，`
- enumeration comma: `、`
- semicolon: `；`
- colon: `：`
- quotation marks: `「」` with nested `『』`
- parenthetical annotation: `（）`
- question/exclamation: `？` / `！`
- dash: `──`
- ellipsis: `……`
- title marks: `《》` and `〈〉`
- interpunct: `．`
- range connector: `—` or `～`
- proper noun mark (專名號／私名號): 橫行標在專名之下（底線形式，如 <u>孫中山</u>、<u>臺灣</u>、<u>教育部</u>）

## High-value rules for assessment reports

1. Use sentence-ending punctuation according to statement/question/exclamation function.
2. Use semicolons to separate parallel clauses when a comma would not clearly show the structure.
3. Use a colon to introduce following material or examples when the preceding text genuinely leads into them.
4. For Chinese quotation structure, use `「」` first and `『』` for a nested quotation when needed.
5. For a complete quoted sentence, the sentence-final punctuation normally stays inside the closing quotation mark. When quoted words are integrated as part of the surrounding sentence, do not mechanically insert punctuation before the closing quotation mark.
6. When quoting Western-language text, follow the punctuation conventions of that language rather than forcing Chinese punctuation.
7. In Chinese prose, use `……` for ellipsis rather than ad hoc sequences of periods.
8. `《》` is primarily used for book/document-level titles and `〈〉` for article/chapter-level titles; judge by the role of the cited work.
9. For time/space/quantity ranges, both `—` and `～` are accepted alternatives in the handbook. Do not call one wrong; prefer report-level consistency.
10. Full-width/half-width symbol consistency (每一處都要抓出來): For Western slashes (e.g. have/has/had to, Yes/No, O/X) or mixed punctuation, flag EVERY SINGLE OCCURRENCE individually rather than using a whole-document summary.
11. Proper noun marks (專名號／私名號標記規範):
    - **適用範疇**：依教育部《重訂標點符號手冊》，專名號用於人名（孫中山、李白）、族名（阿美族）、國名（中華民國、美國）、地名（臺灣、臺北市、基隆市）、機構名（教育部、國立臺灣大學）、路線名（中山高速公路、國道一號）。
    - **官銜職稱嚴格排除**：專名號只標於專名本體，不得連帶標註官銜、職稱或通稱（如：⭕`<u>孫中山</u>先生`、`<u>李</u>校長`、`<u>楊</u>醫生`；❌`<u>孫中山先生</u>`、`<u>李校長</u>`、`<u>楊醫生</u>`）。
    - **標點符號溢標防呆**：專名號不得延伸至相鄰的標點符號（如：⭕`<u>臺北市</u>、`；❌`<u>臺北市、</u>`）。
    - **試題題幹與選項內部一致性**：同一試題中若多個選項或題幹出現相同專名，必須保持一致標註狀態。嚴防「選項①漏標，選項②③④有標」之局部疏漏斷層（例如鸚鵡名字「繽繽」）。
    - **全卷巨觀政策一致性**：現代公文與學術結案報告若全卷正文不使用專名號，前言背景段落即不應突兀保留少數底線；若決定採用，應全卷統一標註，避免「第壹章標註縣市名、後續章節全部未標」之雙軌矛盾。

## Decision rules

- Clear functional error: correct it.
- Officially allowed alternative: accept it; raise only a useful consistency comment.
- If punctuation changes meaning, answer validity, logical relationship, or scope, escalate from copyedit to substantive review.
- Do not alter formulas, statistical notation, code, URLs, identifiers, English passages, or verbatim item/source quotations merely for house style.

## Source fidelity

A reproduced assessment item or direct quotation is source evidence. Preserve it by default. If the item itself is within the review scope and punctuation materially affects item clarity or validity, review the issue explicitly as item quality rather than silently normalizing it.

## Comment economy

Do not comment on every harmless punctuation variation. For a recurring pattern, use one representative whole-document consistency note unless a specific occurrence changes meaning or causes a local reading problem.
