# English curriculum orientation

Primary source bundled in this Skill: `curriculum-english-2018.pdf` (十二年國民基本教育課程綱要－語文領域－英語文，民國107年4月).

Use this orientation to recognize the structure of the official curriculum during general review. For an exact code, stage, wording, or learning-content claim, inspect the bundled official PDF rather than relying only on this summary.

## Broad structure

The official curriculum describes learning focus through learning performance and learning content.

Learning-performance categories include:

- listening;
- speaking;
- reading;
- writing;
- integrated application of listening/speaking/reading/writing;
- learning interest and attitude;
- learning methods and strategies;
- cultural understanding;
- logical thinking, judgment, and creativity.

Stage coding includes:

- II: elementary Grades 3-4;
- III: elementary Grades 5-6;
- IV: junior-high Grades 7-9;
- V: senior-high Grades 10-12.

Some learning performances are marked as higher-order (*) or recurring across stages (◎).

## General-review use

- recognize English stage/category codes when present;
- do not import mathematics curriculum-code logic;
- review item/report consistency, objective, option reasoning, statistics, teaching linkage, and teacher readability first;
- verify a concrete English curriculum claim against `curriculum-english-2018.pdf`;
- use `english-handbook-orientation.md` as secondary interpretation when a performance's scope or stage progression is relevant;
- do not turn a general review into an exhaustive code-by-code curriculum audit unless the user explicitly asks for it.
