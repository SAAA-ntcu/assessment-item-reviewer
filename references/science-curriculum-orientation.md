# Science curriculum orientation

Primary source bundled in this Skill: `curriculum-science-2018.pdf` (十二年國民基本教育課程綱要－自然科學領域，民國107年11月).

Use this orientation to recognize the structure of the official curriculum during general review. For an exact code, stage, wording, or learning-content claim, inspect the bundled official PDF rather than relying only on this summary.

## Broad structure

The official curriculum organizes learning focus around:

- science cognition;
- inquiry ability;
- scientific attitudes and nature of science;
- learning content.

Learning stages include:

- II: elementary Grades 3-4;
- III: elementary Grades 5-6;
- IV: junior-high Grades 7-9;
- V: senior-high.

At elementary level, science is organized as an integrated field. At junior-high level, learning content becomes more disciplinary while still including cross-disciplinary inquiry and connections.

Inquiry-related learning-performance structures include areas such as observing/defining problems, planning/executing, analyzing/discovering, and discussing/communicating.
